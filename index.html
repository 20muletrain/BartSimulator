Pick your shift

Four sims about the Bay Area: run its trains, its cranes and its weather, or fly under the Golden Gate. All are free and run in your browser.

BORT OPS

Run the BORT rail system through a full service day.

  • Set how many trains and cars run on each line
  • Keep commute crowds moving when stations fill up
  • Balance fares and service against the budget
Play BORT OPS

Port New Oakland

Run a ship-to-shore container crane, 174 feet over the Oakland waterfront.

  • Plan the stow, then work every box yourself
  • Land 40-foot containers through wind and swing
  • Finish before the ship's sailing time

Keyboard or touch. Turn phones sideways.

Play Port New Oakland

Can You Fly Under the Golden Gate?

Take off over Alcatraz and thread the 4,200‑foot main span, 220 feet above the water.

  • Pick a Cessna 152, a B-24 or a Boeing 747
  • Fly through fog, a golden sunset, city lights or a storm
  • Miss the ships, the swells and the deck

Keyboard, gamepad or touch. Turn phones sideways.

Play Golden Gate

Upstream

Set the Pacific's drivers and watch the Bay Area's fog, storms and snowpack answer.

  • Warm the ocean, wake El Niño or stall an atmospheric river
  • Replay 1862, 1997, 2017 or 2023, then change them
  • Chase snow on Diablo or fog in Walnut Creek

Real terrain and NOAA climate normals.

Play Upstream
